Collection methodology

  1. Discovery — hourly: the full instrument list of each venue via the filters above → diff against the registry → new instruments get listing_seen_at and an Events entry; missing for 5 consecutive polls → delisted (partial responses do not count).
  2. Ticker snap — every 60 s: mid, 24h vol (in USD at the margin-currency rate), OI, top-of-book spread. One pass = a batch of requests behind a per-domain rate limiter.
  3. Funding — every 5 min, normalized to an 8h interval (venues use 1h/4h/8h — we convert, otherwise the numbers are incomparable).
  4. Depth — hourly, in a full pass spread over time (not a burst): L2 book → notional within ±10/25/50 bps of mid, bid and ask separately. For AMMs — computed from the pool curve (x*y=k / CLMM ranges), not from a book.
  5. Basis — against the last official NYSE/Nasdaq close from reference; during market hours we do not compute against live NBBO (no feed), close only.
  6. Reference — 1/day via Massive: cap buckets mega>$200B / large / mid / small / micro<$300M; non-US underlyings sit outside the buckets.
  7. Health — age of the last successful snap per connector; >15 min of silence → status stale in /api/health.

Metric calculation

ADV 7/30daverage vol over daily rollups (rollup at 00:10 UTC from minute snaps)
spread medianmedian across all snaps over 24h (not the last snap)
depth@±25bpsdaily = average of 24 hourly snapshots; bid/ask separately
funding 7daverage 8h-normalized rate × 3 × 365 → %/yr
vol/OIflow-type marker: >5 — intraday, <1 — positional
Σ per assetsum over all instruments on the underlying across all categories; basis — vol-weighted

Storage

DuckDB venues.duckdb + parquet partitions data/snapshots/YYYY-MM-DD/. Minute snaps kept 30 days, hourly and daily — forever. ~1.5 GB/mo. Service API: /api/venues · /api/venue/{id} · /api/asset/{ticker} · /api/coverage · /api/history/{instrument} · /api/health.

Known limitations

· 24h volumes on CEXes are self-reported (wash trading is not filtered out; we treat depth as the primary metric) · AMM depth is model-derived, not executable · overnight basis is vs the close, not NBBO · Gemini/Bitrue API coverage unconfirmed · funding on venues with a dynamic interval is approximated
